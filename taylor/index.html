Taylor: AI construction estimating and takeoff

Taylor reads every sheet.

Taylor reads every sheet. She handles estimating, drawings and specs. Give her the full bid package and she produces an estimate with every assumption and source shown. When information is missing, she says UNKNOWN instead of guessing.

Book a project review ↗

What Taylor does on your project

  • Reads the drawings, specs, addenda, wage schedule and bid form
  • Performs takeoffs and flags conflicts between sheets and specs
  • Builds labour, material and sub pricing line by line
  • Handles prevailing-wage and public-work requirements
  • Compares sub quotes against her own numbers
  • Issues the estimate with assumptions, sources and revision history
"Never invents a price. Says UNKNOWN when information is missing and always shows the logic."
Her states
UnknownShowing the workRevising

UNKNOWN is deliberate: it names what's missing and who has it. It is never a blank.

Every line shows its source

Each estimate line carries its quantity, unit, the sheet it came from and the basis for the price, such as a wage schedule line times the takeoff plus waste. You can trace any number back to the drawing set.

UNKNOWN on purpose

If something isn't in the set, the line reads UNKNOWN, with the reason and who has the missing item, e.g. "fixture schedule not in the set, request from architect." The estimate total is withheld until every UNKNOWN is resolved. Taylor never fills a gap with a guess, and she drafts the request to the architect for your approval.

Revisions with a reason

When scope moves or an addendum lands, the estimate is revised and the reason is recorded in the revision history.

What Taylor doesn't do

Taylor doesn't stamp drawings or act as your architect. She reads drawings and specs, does takeoffs, flags conflicts and, later, will help with markups and constructability review. Licensed, stamped design responsibility stays with your architect or engineer of record.
