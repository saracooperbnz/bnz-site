What "AI estimating software" means here

Most tools sold as AI estimating are really AI takeoff: they measure areas, lengths and counts on plans. A smaller group also prices the estimate. Fewer still touch sub bids: invitations, follow-up, bid leveling, and COIs. When you compare estimating software for general contractors, check which of those three jobs each tool actually does.

Togal.AI

What it is: cloud takeoff that uses AI to measure areas, walls and counts on PDF plans.
Price: Growth is $299/month per user, billed yearly. Business is custom-priced for 4+ users and adds SSO, onboarding and a dedicated rep (Togal, Oct 16, 2026).
Fits: GCs who need quick quantities and finish trades working off clean architectural sets.
Limits: A 2026 peer-reviewed study found results within 5% of On-Screen Takeoff on floor plans after manual adjustment, with weaker results on scanned drawings (ASCE). Its pricing page lists takeoff features, not estimate pricing or sub outreach. More in our Togal review.

Kreo

What it is: takeoff and estimating software. Essential has no AI. Pro adds AI takeoff (rooms, walls, doors, windows), auto symbol counting and an AI assistant called Caddie. Pricing and live-linked estimates are included (Kreo, Oct 16, 2026).
Price: Essential $100, Pro $175 (1–4 users), Business $250 (5–20 users, from $15,000/yr) per user per month, billed annually. The page says there's no monthly subscription. 7-day free trial and a 7-day money-back guarantee. Paid pilots are credited to an annual plan.
Fits: estimating teams that want takeoff and a priced estimate in one tool, with a published price.
Limits: annual billing only. Sub bids and invitations aren't listed. We found no independent accuracy review to cite as of Oct 16, 2026.

Beam AI (Attentive.ai)

What it is: AI takeoffs, either do-it-yourself software or "done for you" by Beam's team with human review (Beam AI, Oct 16, 2026).
Price: DIY from $8K per trade per year (HVAC, plumbing, steel). Done-for-you from $25K per year, based on trade and volume. Aerial-image takeoffs from $10K per trade per year. Its blog says GC, civil, concrete or earthwork licenses go up to $25K a year (Beam AI blog).
Fits: firms that would rather outsource takeoff than run software, and are bidding steady volume.
Limits: annual licenses are a larger upfront commitment. Beam claims "99%+ accurate" done-for-you takeoffs. That's a vendor claim we couldn't check independently.

Bobyard

What it is: AI takeoff with models trained per trade, flowing into an estimate table, so there's no export step (Bobyard, read Oct 16, 2026).
Price: not published. Bobyard describes a flat per-estimator-seat subscription, a "premium subscription priced for serious commercial bid volume," with a customer success manager included (Bobyard). Ask sales for a quote.
Fits: trade contractors (Bobyard names landscaping, electrical and finishes) and teams that want takeoff and estimate in one workflow.
Limits: no public price. Confirm it covers your trades before you buy.

Handoff

What it is: AI estimating for residential builders and remodelers, with costs Handoff says update daily by US city (Handoff, Oct 16, 2026).
Price: Flex $119, Pro $239, Scale $719 per month, billed annually with a 12-month commitment on Pro and Scale. Scale adds AI takeoffs from residential plans up to 7,000 sq ft and a subcontractor portal.
Fits: residential GCs, custom builders and remodelers.
Limits: built for residential. Commercial GCs should look elsewhere. Ask whether Flex carries the same 12-month commitment.

How to choose

  1. Name the bottleneck. Takeoff hours, pricing, or chasing subs?
  2. Test on your own drawings, including a scanned set and your hardest trade.
  3. Check what it exports to. Your estimate has to end up somewhere your team trusts.
  4. Get the price in writing, per user and per year, with renewal terms.
  5. Count the whole bid, not just takeoff. Sub coverage and bid leveling decide bid day as much as quantities do.

When you're comparing commercial construction estimating software, a short pilot on one live bid tells you more than any roundup, this one included.

FAQ

Asked on every call.

What is the best AI estimating software for general contractors? +
It depends on the bottleneck. Togal and Kreo are takeoff tools your estimators run. Beam AI offers done-for-you takeoffs. Bobyard pairs AI takeoff with an estimate table. Handoff targets residential builders and remodelers. BuildWithBNZ is an AI crew that builds the estimate and runs sub outreach inside Google Workspace and Notion.
How much does AI estimating software cost? +
Published prices as of Oct 16, 2026 range from Handoff's Flex plan at $119/month billed annually to Beam AI done-for-you licenses starting at $25K per year. Togal lists $299/month per user billed yearly; Kreo lists $100 to $250 per user per month billed annually. Several vendors quote by sales.
Can AI replace an estimator? +
Not today. Independent studies of AI takeoff found results close to manual takeoff on clean floor plans but still needing human review, and weaker on scanned drawings. Treat AI as a way to get more bids out, with an estimator checking the numbers.
