# Bid due dates, COI expirations and quote promises in Google Calendar

**The short answer:** Sara, BuildWithBNZ's operations agent, holds the dates that decide whether a bid goes in clean: the bid due date, each sub's promised quote date, and COI expirations. She keeps them on your Google Calendar against the rest of your week, and brings you the ones that need a decision.

## The dates that actually hurt

Most missed bids aren't missed because nobody knew the due date. They're missed because three smaller dates slipped first:

- The electrical sub said "Thursday" for a quote and nobody wrote it down.
- An addendum moved the bid date and only one person saw the email.
- A sub's COI expired the week they mobilized.

Each of those lives in someone's head or in a buried email. Sara pulls them onto the calendar.

## What Sara holds on the calendar

- **Bid due date and time,** with the time zone, from the bid documents.
- **Sub quote promises** that Emma collects from replies ("we'll have it to you Wednesday").
- **COI expiration dates** from certificates Emma collects. See our [COI tracking guide](/guides/subcontractor-coi-tracking/).

## How it fits the week

Sara runs the **Today** board: what needs you, what's in progress, what's done. Calendar dates feed it. When a quote promise passes with no quote, Emma's follow-up is drafted and waiting on your approval. When a COI is getting close to expiring, the renewal request is drafted.

Every Monday, the report lists what's waiting on you and what's waiting on subs, by name.

## What you control

- **Read access first.** The connection starts read-only.
- **Nothing goes to another person without approval.**
- **Scope.** Access follows the scope document for that project.

## Works with the rest of the crew

- **Gmail:** quote promises and COIs come from the replies Emma reads. See [Gmail](/integrations/gmail/).
- **Google Drive:** the bid due date comes from the bid documents Taylor reads. See [Google Drive](/integrations/google-drive/).
- **Notion:** project notes and the sub network. See [Notion](/integrations/notion/).

## FAQ

**What does BuildWithBNZ put on my Google Calendar?**
Bid due dates, COI expirations and the dates subs promised quotes.

**Will it book meetings for me?**
Anything that goes out to another person waits for your approval.

**Why track COI expirations on a calendar?**
So you have time to request a renewal before a sub on site is uncovered.
