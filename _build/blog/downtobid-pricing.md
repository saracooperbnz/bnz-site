# Downtobid pricing and reviews: an honest look

*Facts checked October 16, 2026. Confirm with Downtobid before you buy.*

**The short answer:** Downtobid is AI bidding software for GCs and subs. It reads plan sets, splits them into trade packages, drafts scope notes and sends invitations from your own email. As of October 16, 2026, its homepage FAQ says the **GC product starts at $149/month for your organization**. For subs, the **leads bid board starts at $119/month** and **full bid management at $299/month**. Subs can bid for free ([downtobid.com FAQ](https://downtobid.com/); confirmed by [ConstructionIndustry.ai](https://www.constructionindustry.ai/tools/preconstruction/downtobid/) and [StatWharf](https://statwharf.com/best/bid-management-software/), both October 2026). It doesn't build your estimate.

## What Downtobid costs

| Who | Product | Starting price (Oct 2026) |
|---|---|---|
| GCs | Bid software, per organization | $149 / month |
| Subs | "Leads" bid board | $119 / month |
| Subs | Full bid management | $299 / month |
| Subs | Planroom access and bid submission | Free |

Source: Downtobid homepage FAQ ([downtobid.com](https://www.downtobid.com/)), as of Oct 16, 2026 (browser-checked). The same figures appear on [ConstructionIndustry.ai](https://www.constructionindustry.ai/tools/preconstruction/downtobid/) and [StatWharf](https://statwharf.com/best/bid-management-software/). Downtobid has no pricing page (/pricing returned 404 on Oct 16, 2026). Some older Downtobid blog posts still show earlier plan prices; those are outdated.

## What you get

From Downtobid's site and third-party summaries (Oct 2026):
- AI reads plan sets, detects trade packages and drafts scope notes before invitations go out ([StatWharf](https://statwharf.com/best/bid-management-software/))
- Personalized invitations to bid sent from your own email or domain ([downtobid.com](https://downtobid.com/))
- CSV import and contact cleanup for moving your existing sub list ([StatWharf](https://statwharf.com/best/bid-management-software/))
- A bid board for subs to track invites and deadlines ([downtobid.com](https://downtobid.com/))

## Who Downtobid fits

- **Commercial GCs** who want bid packages and invitations built faster and sent from their own email ([StatWharf](https://statwharf.com/best/bid-management-software/)).
- **Buyers who want a published price.** Unlike BuildingConnected, Downtobid lists starting prices (see our [BuildingConnected pricing](/blog/buildingconnected-pricing/) post).
- **Subs** who want a cheaper bid board, or who just need to bid free when a GC invites them.

## FAQ

**How much does Downtobid cost?**
From $149/month per GC organization. Subs: $119/month (leads) or $299/month (full bid management). As of Oct 16, 2026, per Downtobid's homepage FAQ.

**Do subcontractors pay to bid on Downtobid?**
No, per its FAQ. Subs can upgrade to a paid bid board.

**Does Downtobid have a pricing page?**
Not at downtobid.com/pricing as of Oct 16, 2026. Prices are in the homepage FAQ.
