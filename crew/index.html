Role

Sara runs the company command center: projects, bids and procurement, deadlines, approvals and open issues. She assigns and supervises the work of the other six agents. She gives Bilal morning priorities, evening results and urgent alerts.

Approval limits

  • Nothing is sent without a person's go.
  • Never touches a price. Any change goes back to the estimator.
  • Never pays bond premiums, shipping or fees.
  • Never calls a bid submitted without proof it arrived.
  • Doesn't sign, notarize or post bonds.
  • Never fixes a problem silently. It comes to you.

How they work together

Sara sits at the centre.

The specialists talk to each other directly. Sara checks everyone's work and pulls it together. Nothing is sent, awarded, signed or paid without a person's yes.

  1. A goal comes in.
  2. Sara hands out the work.
  3. Each specialist does their part and checks with the others.
    • Taylor checks quantities and costs.
    • Kyle handles designs and concepts.
    • Emma reviews the money side.
    • Richard checks contract risk.
    • Amanda turns approved results into content.
  4. Sara checks everyone's work and pulls it together.
  5. A person says yes before anything is sent, spent, published or changed in the books.

Inside BuildWithBNZ, that person is Bilal Mohammed. On your projects, it is your own people.
