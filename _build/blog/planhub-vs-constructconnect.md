# PlanHub vs ConstructConnect for finding bids

*Prices checked October 16, 2026 (ET). Confirm with each vendor before you buy.*

**The short answer:** **PlanHub** is a bidding network. GCs post projects and collect bids **free**, and subs pay for wider lead search, starting at **$1,999/year for a 50-mile radius** ([PlanHub sub pricing](https://planhub.com/pricing-subcontractors/)). **ConstructConnect** sells project intelligence: leads, bid documents and planholder lists, priced **per market and per seat**, from **$129/month** ([ConstructConnect pricing](https://www.constructconnect.com/pricing)). GCs posting their own bids usually start with PlanHub. Subs and suppliers hunting for projects, including public and planning-stage work, often compare both.

## Side-by-side

| | PlanHub | ConstructConnect |
|---|---|---|
| What it is | Bid network + bid management | Project leads, documents and intelligence (Project Intelligence); separate takeoff product |
| GC cost | Essentials free; Pro and Premier prices not published ([GC pricing page](https://planhub.com/pricing-general-contractors/)) | Same plans as everyone (per market, per seat) |
| Sub cost | Free profile to receive invites and bid within a local radius; Premier $1,999 (50 mi), $2,349 (100 mi), $3,299 (200 mi) per year ([sub pricing page](https://planhub.com/pricing-subcontractors/)) | Starter $129/mo per market (month-to-month); Professional from $199/mo per market per seat, billed annually; Premium from $10,000/yr |
| Priced by | Subs: radius around your business, plus trade ([sub pricing page](https://planhub.com/pricing-subcontractors/)); GC paid tiers: see [PlanHub's GC pricing page](https://planhub.com/pricing-general-contractors/) | Market and seat |
| Contract | Annual Premier subscription (subs) | Starter is month-to-month; others are annual (quarterly or annual billing); no mid-term refunds |
| Takeoff | GC add-on, or included on GC Premier ([GC pricing page](https://planhub.com/pricing-general-contractors/)) | Sold separately: Takeoff Starter $1,200/yr (1 seat), Professional $2,200/yr first seat, $1,700 each additional |

Sources (all read Oct 16, 2026): [PlanHub sub pricing](https://planhub.com/pricing-subcontractors/), [PlanHub GC pricing](https://planhub.com/pricing-general-contractors/), [ConstructConnect pricing](https://www.constructconnect.com/pricing).

## PlanHub: who it fits

- **GCs who want more bids on their own projects without paying.** PlanHub says GCs get free, unlimited project posting, file sharing, messaging to many subs at once, basic bid leveling and directory search ([PlanHub GC pricing](https://planhub.com/pricing-general-contractors/)).
- **Subs who bid for GCs in a set area.** Premier is a flat annual price set by your radius and trade, not by how many bids you submit ([PlanHub sub pricing](https://planhub.com/pricing-subcontractors/)).

**Limits:**
- GC Pro and Premier prices aren't published. PlanHub says a specialist sets the price on a call ([PlanHub GC pricing](https://planhub.com/pricing-general-contractors/)).
- Radius pricing means a wider territory costs more. Sub Enterprise (national reach, teams of 3+) has no listed price; see [PlanHub's pricing page](https://planhub.com/pricing-subcontractors/).
- **Reviews:** we didn't find independent reviews we could summarize fairly by the check date. [VERIFY: check G2/Capterra "planhub reviews" before publishing]

## ConstructConnect: who it fits

- **Subs, suppliers and GCs hunting for projects** they weren't invited to. Professional adds bid documents, planholder lists, contact info, planning-stage projects, daily saved searches and AI document search ([ConstructConnect pricing](https://www.constructconnect.com/pricing); feature list per [ConstructionBids.ai](https://constructionbids.ai/blog/constructconnect-pricing-guide-2026), verified Oct 6, 2026).
- **Buyers who want a month-to-month option.** Starter is month-to-month. Everything else is annual ([ConstructConnect FAQ](https://www.constructconnect.com/pricing)).

**Limits:**
- **Per market, per seat** adds up. Three metros means three subscriptions ([ColdOutreach.ai analysis](https://coldoutreach.ai/blog/construction-lead-generation-cost), checked Sep 18, 2026).
- No refunds for mid-term cancellation ([ConstructConnect FAQ](https://www.constructconnect.com/pricing)).
- Premium's $10,000/yr floor was published around Oct 5, 2026; before that it was custom-priced ([PricingSaaS Pulse](https://pricingsaas.com/companies/constructconnect/diffs/2026W41)). Expect changes.
- BidClerk and CMD now redirect to ConstructConnect, so old BidClerk prices are out of date ([ColdOutreach.ai](https://coldoutreach.ai/blog/construction-lead-generation-cost)).

## Quick checklist: which one?

- [ ] **Are you a GC posting your own bids?** Start with PlanHub's free tier.
- [ ] **Are you a sub who wants more invites in one area?** Price PlanHub Premier for your radius.
- [ ] **Do you need projects you're not invited to,** including planning-stage and public work? Look at ConstructConnect Professional.
- [ ] **Do you cover several metros?** Multiply ConstructConnect by markets and seats before comparing.
- [ ] **Do you need takeoff too?** Check PlanHub's add-on versus ConstructConnect Takeoff pricing.
- [ ] **Get the quote in writing,** with term and renewal.

## FAQ

**Is PlanHub free?**
For GCs posting projects, yes (Essentials). Subs get a free profile; Premier starts at $1,999/year (50 mi).

**How much does ConstructConnect cost?**
From $129/month per market (Starter), from $199/month per market per seat billed annually (Professional), and from $10,000/year (Premium), as of Oct 16, 2026.

**What's the main difference?**
PlanHub is a bidding network. ConstructConnect is project intelligence, priced per market and seat.
