Downtobid pricing and reviews: an honest look

Facts checked October 16, 2026. Confirm with Downtobid before you buy.

The short answer: Downtobid is AI bidding software for GCs and subs. It reads plan sets, splits them into trade packages, drafts scope notes and sends invitations from your own email. As of October 16, 2026, its homepage FAQ says the GC product starts at $149/month for your organization. For subs, the leads bid board starts at $119/month and full bid management at $299/month. Subs can bid for free (downtobid.com FAQ; confirmed by ConstructionIndustry.ai and StatWharf, both October 2026). It doesn't build your estimate.

What Downtobid costs

Who Product Starting price (Oct 2026)
GCs Bid software, per organization $149 / month
Subs "Leads" bid board $119 / month
Subs Full bid management $299 / month
Subs Planroom access and bid submission Free

Source: Downtobid homepage FAQ (downtobid.com), as of Oct 16, 2026 (browser-checked). The same figures appear on ConstructionIndustry.ai and StatWharf. Downtobid has no pricing page (/pricing returned 404 on Oct 16, 2026). Some older Downtobid blog posts still show earlier plan prices; those are outdated.

These are starting prices. The FAQ doesn't say how contract terms or add-ons work, so ask.

What you get

From Downtobid's site and third-party summaries (Oct 2026):

  • AI reads plan sets, detects trade packages and drafts scope notes before invitations go out (StatWharf)
  • Personalized invitations to bid sent from your own email or domain (downtobid.com)
  • CSV import and contact cleanup for moving your existing sub list (StatWharf)
  • A bid board for subs to track invites and deadlines (downtobid.com)

Who Downtobid fits

  • Commercial GCs who want bid packages and invitations built faster and sent from their own email (StatWharf).
  • Buyers who want a published price. Unlike BuildingConnected, Downtobid lists starting prices (see our BuildingConnected pricing post).
  • Subs who want a cheaper bid board, or who just need to bid free when a GC invites them.

Reviews and limits

As of Oct 16, 2026 we didn't find enough independent reviews to summarize fairly, so we won't.

What we can say from Downtobid's own descriptions:

  • It's a bidding tool, not an estimator. It drafts scope notes and invitations. The descriptions we found don't list building a priced estimate.

Where BuildWithBNZ fits instead

Downtobid and BuildWithBNZ, an AI estimating and bidding crew, overlap on sub invitations from your own email. The difference is the estimate:

  • Taylor reads the drawings, specs, addenda, wage schedule and bid form, does the takeoff, flags conflicts, and prices labor, material and subs line by line, with UNKNOWN where information is missing.
