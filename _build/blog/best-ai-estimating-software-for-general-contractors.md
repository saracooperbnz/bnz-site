# Best AI estimating software for general contractors (2026)

*Prices and features checked October 16, 2026 (ET). BuildWithBNZ publishes this page and is one of the options listed. We've tried to be fair: every competitor price links to its source, and anything we couldn't confirm is marked.*

**The short answer:** There's no single best tool. Pick by your bottleneck:
- **Need faster takeoff your estimators run themselves?** Togal or Kreo.
- **Want takeoffs done for you?** Beam AI (Attentive.ai).
- **Want AI takeoff and an estimate in one place, by trade?** Bobyard.
- **Residential builder or remodeler?** Handoff.
- **Need the estimate built *and* the subs chased, inside Google Workspace?** [BuildWithBNZ, an AI estimating crew for general contractors](/).

## What "AI estimating" means here

Most tools sold as AI estimating are really **AI takeoff**: they measure areas, lengths and counts on plans. A smaller group also **prices** the estimate. Fewer still touch **sub bids**: invitations, follow-up, [bid leveling](/guides/bid-leveling/), and COIs. When you compare estimating software for general contractors, check which of those three jobs each tool actually does.

## Togal.AI

**What it is:** cloud takeoff that uses AI to measure areas, walls and counts on PDF plans.
**Price:** Growth is $299/month per user, billed yearly. Business is custom-priced for 4+ users and adds SSO, onboarding and a dedicated rep ([Togal](https://www.togal.ai/pricing), Oct 16, 2026).
**Fits:** GCs who need quick quantities and finish trades working off clean architectural sets.
**Limits:** A 2026 peer-reviewed study found results within 5% of On-Screen Takeoff on floor plans after manual adjustment, with weaker results on scanned drawings ([ASCE](https://ascelibrary.org/doi/10.1061/9780784486986.086)). Its pricing page lists takeoff features, not estimate pricing or sub outreach. More in [our Togal review](/blog/togal-ai-review/).

## Kreo

**What it is:** a takeoff and estimating tool. Essential has no AI. Pro adds AI takeoff (rooms, walls, doors, windows), auto symbol counting and an AI assistant called Caddie. Pricing and live-linked estimates are included ([Kreo](https://www.kreo.net/pricing), Oct 16, 2026).
**Price:** see [Kreo's pricing page](https://www.kreo.net/pricing) for current plans and terms; we haven't confirmed them.
**Fits:** estimating teams that want takeoff and a priced estimate in one tool, with a published price.
**Limits:** annual billing only. Sub bids and invitations aren't listed. We found no independent accuracy review to cite as of Oct 16, 2026.

## Beam AI (Attentive.ai)

**What it is:** AI takeoffs, either do-it-yourself software or "done for you" by Beam's team with human review ([Beam AI](https://www.ibeam.ai/pricing), Oct 16, 2026).
**Price:** sold as annual licenses by trade and volume; see [Beam AI's pricing page](https://www.ibeam.ai/pricing) for current figures, which we haven't confirmed.
**Fits:** firms that would rather outsource takeoff than run software, and are bidding steady volume.
**Limits:** annual licenses are a larger upfront commitment. Beam claims "99%+ accurate" done-for-you takeoffs. That's a vendor claim we couldn't check independently.

## Bobyard

**What it is:** AI takeoff with models trained per trade, flowing into an estimate table, so there's no export step ([Bobyard](https://www.bobyard.com/blogs/bobyard-vs-togal-ai/), read Oct 16, 2026).
**Price:** not published. Bobyard describes a flat per-estimator-seat subscription, a "premium subscription priced for serious commercial bid volume," with a customer success manager included ([Bobyard](https://www.bobyard.com/blogs/bobyard-vs-takeoff-monkey/)). Ask sales for a quote.
**Fits:** trade contractors (Bobyard names landscaping, electrical and finishes) and teams that want takeoff and estimate in one workflow.
**Limits:** no public price. Confirm it covers your trades before you buy.

## Handoff

**What it is:** AI estimating for residential builders and remodelers, with costs Handoff says update daily by US city ([Handoff](https://www.handoff.ai/pricing), Oct 16, 2026).
**Price:** see [Handoff's pricing page](https://www.handoff.ai/pricing) for current plans and terms; we haven't confirmed them.
**Fits:** residential GCs, custom builders and remodelers.
**Limits:** built for residential. Commercial GCs should look elsewhere. Ask about contract terms before you buy.

## How to choose

1. **Name the bottleneck.** Takeoff hours, pricing, or chasing subs?
2. **Test on your own drawings,** including a scanned set and your hardest trade.
3. **Check what it exports to.** Your estimate has to end up somewhere your team trusts.
4. **Get the price in writing,** per user and per year, with renewal terms.
5. **Count the whole bid,** not just takeoff. Sub coverage and [bid leveling](/guides/bid-leveling/) decide bid day as much as quantities do.

When you're weighing an AI construction estimator, a short pilot on one live bid tells you more than any roundup, this one included.
