Why your own inbox matters

Subs answer people they know. An invitation from your address, with your name on it, gets read differently than a notification from a portal they've never logged into. Your own inbox also keeps the whole conversation in one place: the invite, the RFI, the quote and the COI.

The catch has always been time. Writing trade-specific invitations, attaching the right sheets, and chasing every sub who hasn't answered is hours of work per bid. That's the part Emma takes on.

What Emma does in Gmail

  • Drafts invitations to bid for each trade, with the right files attached from your project folder.
  • Puts every draft in Sara's approvals queue. You approve, edit or send it back.
  • Follows up until a quote, a no-bid or a named reason comes back. "Waiting" always has a name attached: a person, a document, a date.
  • Collects quotes, COIs, W-9s and compliance documents from the replies.
  • Levels every scope against Taylor's estimate once quotes come in.
  • Remembers who performs: who answers on time and whose paperwork is clean.

What you control

  • Every send. Nothing goes to a sub without your approval.
  • The wording. Edit any draft before it goes.
  • Read access first. The connection starts read-only.
  • Scope. Access follows the scope document for that project.

A day with Emma in your inbox (sample, not a customer)

Electrical package, three subs invited:

  • Sub A: quote received, COI and W-9 in, scope leveled. Next action: none, ready for your review.
  • Sub B: no quote yet, scope gap of two items. Next action: second follow-up drafted, waiting on your approval.
  • Sub C: quote received, COI expires Friday. Next action: request COI renewal.

FAQ

Asked on every call.

Does BuildWithBNZ send email without my approval? +
No. Emma drafts bid invitations and follow-ups in your Gmail. Each one waits in the approvals queue until you approve, edit or send it back.
Who do the emails come from? +
From your own Gmail, so subs see your name and your company, not a third-party bidding portal.
Do subs need an account? +
Subs receive normal email from you and can reply the way they always do.
Does it work with Outlook? +
Not yet. Microsoft 365 (Outlook and OneDrive) is listed as Next. Today the crew works in Gmail.
