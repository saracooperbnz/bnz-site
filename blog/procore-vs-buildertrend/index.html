Procore vs Buildertrend: which fits your company?

Checked October 16, 2026 (ET). Neither vendor publishes prices, so your quote decides.

Procore vs Buildertrend, the short answer: Both are construction management platforms with unlimited users, and neither publishes a price. Procore charges an annual fee per product, scaled to your Annual Construction Volume (ACV) (Procore pricing). It's the common pick for commercial GCs running many stakeholders on one project. Buildertrend gives a custom quote from a short questionnaire (Buildertrend pricing) and is often considered by residential builders and remodelers. Pick on who your clients are and how much construction you run, then compare written quotes.

Procore: what to know

  • ACV is the meter. Procore defines it as "the aggregate dollar value of the construction work across your projects" (Procore). Price goes up as volume goes up.
  • Unlimited users means you can bring in subs, owners and architects without paying per seat.
  • Ways to manage lumpy volume: multi-year pools, pre-priced extra volume and locked renewal rates (Procore blog).
  • Limits:
  • No public price, so you can't compare without a sales call.
  • It's priced by product, so adding modules (for example estimating or bid management) adds cost.
  • TrustRadius lists no free version or trial (TrustRadius).

Buildertrend: what to know

  • Custom quote. Buildertrend's page asks "a few quick questions," then you schedule a call (Buildertrend).
  • A reviewer's 2026 quote included a one-year minimum and a paid support tier (Connecteam, 2026). Ask whether a paid support tier is required for you.
  • Small-company fit: the same reviewer said picking the $0–$499,000 volume option got a message that Buildertrend may not be the best fit for smaller businesses (Connecteam).
  • Limits:
  • No public price.
  • Reported annual minimum.

Quick checklist

  • [ ] Commercial with lots of outside stakeholders? Get a Procore quote.
  • [ ] Residential builder or remodeler with homeowner clients? Get a Buildertrend quote.
  • [ ] Know your annual construction volume before either call. Both ask about it.
  • [ ] List the modules you actually need. Procore prices per product.
  • [ ] Ask about term, renewal increases and required support fees, in writing.
  • [ ] Still writing the weekly owner report by hand? Neither platform writes it for you; see construction weekly report automation.
  • [ ] Check where estimating and sub paperwork live. Tracking subs (see subcontractor compliance software) may need its own process either way.
