# Roofing estimating software: what to look for and 7 tools to compare

*Checked October 20, 2026 (ET). Prices come from each vendor's own site on that date. BuildWithBNZ publishes this page.*

**The short answer:** Good roofing estimating software turns a roof into a price fast. It gets you accurate measurements, adds pitch and waste, builds the material list, pulls in supplier prices and sends a clean proposal. Some tools only measure. Some measure and estimate. Others run your whole roofing business. Pick based on how you sell: retail reroofs, insurance work or commercial bids. Then test one or two on a roof you've already priced.

A few terms first:

- A **square** is 100 square feet of roof. Shingles and labor are usually priced by the square.
- **Pitch** is how steep the roof is. It's written as rise over 12 inches, like 6/12. Steeper roofs need more material and more labor time.
- A **waste factor** is extra material you add for cuts, hips, valleys and starter rows.
- An **aerial measurement report** is a roof report built from aerial or satellite images. It lists areas, pitches, edges and facets.
- A **facet** is one flat plane of the roof. More facets usually means a more complex roof.
- A **material list** is every item you need to order: shingles, underlayment, drip edge, flashing, vents and more.
- A **supplement** is a request to an insurance company to add items that were left out of its estimate for a claim.

## What roofing estimating software should do

Use this checklist in every demo.

**1. Measurements.** Can it order aerial reports, or let you draw the roof yourself? How long do reports take? Can you measure on site with a phone?

**2. Pitch and waste.** Does it apply pitch to each facet? Can you set your own waste factor by roof type?

**3. Squares to materials.** Does it turn squares and edges into a full material list automatically?

**4. Supplier pricing.** Can you load your supplier's prices or connect to a distributor? Can you place the order from the tool?

**5. Proposals.** Can you send a branded proposal with options (good, better, best) and get an e-signature?

**6. Insurance work.** If you do storm or restoration work, can it export files your adjuster or supplement team can use?

**7. Mobile.** Does your sales rep need a roofing estimating app on the phone, or will a laptop work?

**8. Pricing model.** Is it per user, per report, per project or a flat monthly fee? Report fees add up on busy months.

## Measurement tools

These focus on getting the roof numbers right.

**Eagleview.** Eagleview sells aerial roof reports built from its own imagery. Its site says it is "built on more than 3.5 billion high-resolution aerial images." Its pricing page ([Eagleview](https://www.eagleview.com/pricing/), Oct 20, 2026) lists Premium roof reports by roof size and account tier. For example, a small roof (20 squares) is listed at $24.25 to $32.75, and a large roof (over 40 squares) at $75.50 to $87. The top tier says "Call for pricing." It also lists gutter, wall and full-house reports.

**Hover.** Hover builds a 3D model and measurements from photos taken on a phone. Its pricing page ([Hover](https://hover.to/pricing/), Oct 20, 2026) lists:
- Starter: pay per project, with your first 3 projects free.
- Pro: $999 a year, with $20 off every project.
- Business: $2,999 a year, with a flat rate per project type.
- Enterprise: custom pricing.

Per-project prices depend on the structure and the type of measurement, so check the page for your roofs.

## Measure, estimate and propose

These add estimates, material lists and proposals on top of measurements.

**RoofSnap.** RoofSnap says it lets you "measure, estimate, & get paid — all in one tool" ([RoofSnap](https://roofsnap.com/)). Its pricing page ([RoofSnap pricing](https://roofsnap.com/pricing/), Oct 20, 2026) lists:
- Pay-as-you-go: measurement reports starting at $13 per order, no subscription.
- Monthly: starting at $105 per user per month, with a 7-day free trial.
- Annual: starting at $52 per user per month for 10 or more users, $61 for 5 to 9 users and $78 for 2 to 4 users.

**Roofr.** Roofr offers measurement reports, proposals, material ordering and a job board. Its pricing page ([Roofr](https://roofr.com/pricing), Oct 20, 2026) lists:
- Starter: $0 a month, with $19 measurement reports and 10 trial proposals.
- Essentials: $249 a month billed monthly, or $209 a month billed yearly.
- Scale: $349 a month billed monthly, or $299 a month billed yearly.

Roofr also lists **ESX files** with its reports. ESX is the file type used by Xactimate, estimating software used for insurance claims.

## Full roofing business platforms

These run sales, production and paperwork, with estimating built in.

**AccuLynx.** AccuLynx says it is "designed for roofing companies that primarily focus on residential insurance restoration and retail roofing." Its site lists supplier catalogs and direct ordering with ABC Supply, QXO and SRS, plus aerial measurement integrations such as Eagleview ([AccuLynx](https://acculynx.com/)). Pricing is not listed. The site asks you to request pricing.

**JobNimbus.** JobNimbus calls itself a roofing CRM. It lists four plans by team size: Essentials (up to 3 users), Pro (up to 10), Premium (up to 19) and Enterprise (20+). All plans include estimates, e-sign, invoices and supplier integrations ([JobNimbus](https://www.jobnimbus.com/pricing), Oct 20, 2026). Pricing is not listed. Each plan says "Request pricing."

## Instant online quotes

**Roofle.** Roofle's RoofQuote PRO puts an instant roof quote tool on your website, so homeowners can get a price before they call. Its plans page ([Roofle](https://offers.roofle.com/plans), Oct 20, 2026) lists a monthly plan at $350 a month plus a $2,000 setup fee, and an annual plan at $5,500 for the first year, which its plan card says includes setup. Confirm setup fees with Roofle before you sign.

## Which type fits how you sell?

| How you sell | Start by looking at |
|---|---|
| Retail reroofs, priced at the kitchen table | A measure-and-propose tool, or instant quotes on your website |
| Insurance and storm work | Tools that list ESX export and supplement support, or a full platform built for restoration |
| A big sales team with many jobs at once | A full roofing business platform |
| Commercial bids from a plan set and specs | A tool that reads the drawings and specs, not just aerial images |

## How to test estimating software for roofing contractors

1. **Pick a roof you already priced.** You know the right answer.
2. **Compare the measurements.** Check squares, pitch and edges against your own numbers.
3. **Load your waste factor and supplier prices.** Don't judge it on default numbers.
4. **Build a real proposal.** Time how long it takes from measurement to sent proposal.
5. **Try it on a phone.** Have a sales rep run the roofing estimating app in the field.
6. **Ask about the term.** Monthly or yearly? Setup fees? Report fees? What happens if you cancel?
