Construction estimating software for small contractors

If you bid a handful of jobs a month, start with a free plan (such as STACK Free; Buildxact also lists one) or the PDF tool you already have. Move to a paid tool when takeoff hours or missed scope start costing you work. Paid plans vary by vendor, plan and seat count (see the price table below), and most lock you into 12 months. Pick by your work type: residential builders and remodelers lean toward Buildxact or Handoff; trade contractors and small commercial GCs lean toward STACK, PlanSwift or Kreo.

What small contractors should check first

A checklist before any demo:

  • Work type fit. Residential remodels, new homes, trade packages or commercial bids? Tools are built for one more than the others.
  • Takeoff and pricing. Some tools only measure. Look for automated construction estimating, where quantities flow into pricing, if you don't want to retype them into Excel.
  • Your own costs. Can you load your labor rates and supplier prices, or are you stuck with a national database?
  • Mac or Windows. PlanSwift is described as Windows-based (ConstructConnect). STACK, Buildxact and Kreo are cloud-based.
  • Term. Almost everything above is a 12-month commitment. Run the free plan or trial on a real bid first.
  • Users. Per-user pricing (STACK, Kreo, PlanSwift) vs. company-wide plans changes the math once a second person estimates.
  • Proposal output. Can you send a clean proposal to the owner straight from the tool?

Quick picks by situation

  • Residential builder or remodeler, 1–3 people: Buildxact or Handoff Flex. Both are built for residential. For Buildxact's current plans, see buildxact.com/us/pricing. Handoff Scale's AI takeoff covers residential plans up to 7,000 sq ft (Handoff).
  • Trade sub bidding a few jobs a month: STACK Free to start, then STACK Pro or ConstructConnect Takeoff Starter.
  • Small commercial GC who prices in Excel: Kreo Essential (takeoff with live-linked estimates, no AI) or a PDF tool like Bluebeam. See PlanSwift vs Bluebeam.

The part software doesn't do

Estimating software makes the estimator faster. It doesn't send sub invitations, chase quotes, collect insurance certificates (see our COI tracking guide) or level sub numbers (see leveling sub bids). In a small company that's often the same person who does the estimate, and it's where nights and weekends go.

FAQ

Asked on every call.

Is there free construction estimating software for small contractors? +
Yes, with limits. As of Oct 16, 2026, STACK has a free plan with one active project per month and up to 50 measurements per project. Buildxact also lists a free plan; see buildxact.com/us/pricing for current terms.
How much does estimating software cost for a small construction business? +
Published prices as of Oct 16, 2026 range from free plans to paid annual plans that vary by vendor, plan and seat count; see the price table in this post for each tool. Most paid plans require a 12-month term.
Do small contractors need estimating software at all? +
If you bid only a few jobs a month, a spreadsheet and PDF measuring tool can work. Software pays off when takeoff time or missed scope starts costing you bids.
