PlanHub vs ConstructConnect for finding bids

Prices checked October 16, 2026 (ET). Confirm with each vendor before you buy.

PlanHub vs ConstructConnect, the short answer: PlanHub is a bidding network. GCs post projects and collect bids free, and subs pay for wider lead search(PlanHub sub pricing). ConstructConnect sells project intelligence: leads, bid documents and planholder lists, priced per market and per seat (ConstructConnect pricing). GCs posting their own bids usually start with PlanHub. Subs and suppliers hunting for projects, including public and planning-stage work, often compare both.

PlanHub vs ConstructConnect side by side

PlanHub: who it fits

  • GCs who want more bids on their own projects without paying. PlanHub says GCs get free, unlimited project posting, file sharing, messaging to many subs at once, basic bid leveling and directory search (PlanHub GC pricing).
  • Subs who bid for GCs in a set area. Premier is a flat annual price set by your radius and trade, not by how many bids you submit (PlanHub sub pricing).

Limits:

  • GC Pro and Premier prices aren't published. PlanHub says a specialist sets the price on a call (PlanHub GC pricing).
  • Radius pricing means a wider territory costs more. Sub Enterprise (national reach, teams of 3+) has no listed price; see PlanHub's pricing page.
  • Reviews: we didn't find independent reviews we could summarize fairly by the check date.

ConstructConnect: who it fits

  • Subs, suppliers and GCs hunting for projects they weren't invited to. Professional adds bid documents, planholder lists, contact info, planning-stage projects, daily saved searches and AI document search (ConstructConnect pricing; feature list per ConstructionBids.ai, verified Oct 6, 2026).
  • Buyers who want a month-to-month option. Starter is month-to-month. Everything else is annual (ConstructConnect FAQ).

Limits:

  • Per market, per seat adds up. Three metros means three subscriptions (ColdOutreach.ai analysis, checked Sep 18, 2026).
  • No refunds for mid-term cancellation (ConstructConnect FAQ).
  • BidClerk and CMD now redirect to ConstructConnect, so old BidClerk prices are out of date (ColdOutreach.ai).

Quick checklist: which one?

  • [ ] Are you a GC posting your own bids? Start with PlanHub's free tier.
  • [ ] Are you a sub who wants more invites in one area? Price PlanHub Premier for your radius.
  • [ ] Do you need projects you're not invited to, including planning-stage and public work? Look at ConstructConnect Professional.
  • [ ] Do you cover several metros? Multiply ConstructConnect by markets and seats before comparing.
  • [ ] Do you need takeoff too? Check PlanHub's add-on versus ConstructConnect Takeoff pricing.
  • [ ] Get the quote in writing, with term and renewal.

Finding bids is half the job

Both tools help you find or post work. The other half still lands on your desk: drafting the invitation to bid, chasing subs, collecting COIs and W-9s, and making sure you level the bids before you choose. That's where most bid-day misses come from, and it's the job subcontractor bid management software is supposed to cover.

Where BuildWithBNZ fits

BuildWithBNZ isn't a lead database or bid network. It's three AI workers that run the bid inside your own Gmail, Google Drive, Google Calendar and Notion:

  • Emma builds your sub list for your trade, area and job size, drafts invitations in your Gmail with the right files, follows up until a quote, no-bid or reason comes back, collects COIs and W-9s, and levels each scope against the estimate.
  • Taylor builds the estimate from the bid package, with sources shown.
  • Sara holds bid dates on your calendar and keeps one approvals queue. Nothing goes out without your approval.

If you're weighing bid management software for general contractors, BNZ is the option where the work happens in your own inbox instead of a portal. It handles the sub side, not lead finding. Some contractors use a lead source and BNZ.

FAQ

Is PlanHub free? For GCs posting projects, yes (Essentials). Subs get a free profile; Premier is paid (see PlanHub's pricing page).

How much does ConstructConnect cost? It's priced per market and per seat; see ConstructConnect's pricing page for current plans.

What's the main difference? PlanHub is a bidding network. ConstructConnect is project intelligence, priced per market and seat.
