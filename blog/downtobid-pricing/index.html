Downtobid pricing and reviews: an honest look

Downtobid is AI bidding software for GCs and subs. It reads plan sets, splits them into trade packages, drafts scope notes and sends invitations from your own email. Its homepage FAQ lists $149/month for the organization (GCs). For subs, the leads bid board starts at $119/month and the full bid management software at $299/month, and subs bid free (Downtobid homepage FAQ, checked Oct 16, 2026). It doesn't build your estimate.

What you get

From Downtobid's site and third-party summaries (Oct 2026):

  • AI reads plan sets, detects trade packages and drafts scope notes before invitations go out (StatWharf)
  • Personalized invitations to bid sent from your own email or domain (downtobid.com)
  • CSV import and contact cleanup for moving your existing sub list (StatWharf)
  • A bid board for subs to track invites and deadlines (downtobid.com)

Who Downtobid fits

  • Commercial GCs who want bid packages and invitations built faster and sent from their own email (StatWharf).
  • Buyers who want a published price. Unlike BuildingConnected, Downtobid lists starting prices (see our BuildingConnected pricing post).
  • Subs who want a cheaper bid board, or who just need to bid free when a GC invites them.

Reviews and limits

As of Oct 16, 2026 we didn't find enough independent reviews to summarize fairly, so we won't.

What we can say from Downtobid's own descriptions:

  • It's a bidding tool, not an estimator. It drafts scope notes and invitations. The descriptions we found don't list building a priced estimate.
  • Pricing mismatch between its FAQ and blog (above) means you should get your price in writing.

Where BuildWithBNZ fits instead

Downtobid and BuildWithBNZ overlap on sub invitations from your own email. The difference is the estimate:

  • Taylor reads the drawings, specs, addenda, wage schedule and bid form, does the takeoff, flags conflicts, and prices labor, material and subs line by line, with UNKNOWN where information is missing.
  • Emma drafts invitations in your Gmail with the right files from your Google Drive, follows up, collects quotes, COIs and W-9s, and levels each scope against Taylor's estimate (see how to level subcontractor bids).
  • Sara keeps one approvals queue and holds bid dates on your Google Calendar. Nothing goes out without your approval.

Pick Downtobid if you want a self-serve bidding tool with a published monthly price, and your estimating is already covered.

Look at BuildWithBNZ if you need the estimate built too, and want the work done in Google Workspace and Notion rather than one more tool your team has to run. See bid management software for general contractors and AI construction estimating software.
