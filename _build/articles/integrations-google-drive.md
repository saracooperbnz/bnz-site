# Google Drive for construction estimating

**The short answer:** If your bid packages already live in Google Drive, you don't have to upload them anywhere. Taylor, BuildWithBNZ's estimating agent, reads the drawing set, specs and addenda from your project folder, does the takeoff, and saves the estimate back to your Workspace as a file you own. Access starts as read-only and is scoped to the project.

## The problem with "upload your plans here"

Most estimating tools want the plans inside their system. So the drawings live in two places: your Drive folder and the vendor's portal. Then addendum 2 lands in Drive, nobody re-uploads it, and the estimate is priced off old sheets.

BNZ works the other way. The project folder in your Drive stays the single source. When the architect's office drops a new addendum in that folder, it's in the same place Taylor reads from.

## What Taylor does with your Drive folder

- **Reads the full package:** drawings, specs, addenda, wage schedule and bid form.
- **Performs takeoffs** and flags conflicts between sheets and specs.
- **Prices line by line:** labor, material and sub pricing, each line traced to a sheet or spec section.
- **Writes UNKNOWN instead of guessing.** If a fixture schedule isn't in the set, the line says so and names who has it (for example, "request from architect").
- **Saves the estimate back** with its assumptions, sources and revision history.

See [Taylor](/taylor/) and [AI construction estimating](/construction-estimating-ai/) for more on how the estimate is built.

## How the connection works

1. **Project Review.** You show us one project. Nothing is connected yet.
2. **Scope in writing.** The scope document says which folders the crew will read.
3. **Connect Drive, read access first.** You grant access to the project folder. Nothing is moved or replaced.
4. **Taylor reads the package** and issues Estimate Rev 1 with its logic attached.
5. **Outputs land in your Workspace:** the estimate, the scope document and the Monday report are files you own.

## What you control

- **Scope of access.** Per project, not your whole Drive.
- **Read first.** The crew starts with read access.
- **Your files stay yours.** If you stop working with us, the files are still in your Drive.

## Works with the rest of the crew

- **Gmail:** Emma attaches the right sheets from the same folder to bid invitations. See [Gmail](/integrations/gmail/).
- **Google Calendar:** Sara holds the bid due date against the week. See [Google Calendar](/integrations/google-calendar/).
- **Notion:** project notes and the sub network update in place. See [Notion](/integrations/notion/).

## Not live yet

Dropbox ("project folders will read in place"), Microsoft 365/OneDrive, Bluebeam and PlanSwift/STACK are listed as **Next**. They are not live today. If your plans live there, tell us at the Project Review.

## FAQ

**Do I have to move my files out of Google Drive?**
No. Taylor reads from your existing project folder.

**Does BuildWithBNZ get access to my whole Drive?**
No. Access is scoped per project and starts as read access.

**Where does the estimate end up?**
In your own Google Workspace, as a file you own.

**Can it read Dropbox or OneDrive folders?**
Not yet. Both are listed as Next.
