What does it cost?

It starts with a Project Review: a real call about one real project. Then we send you the scope and one set price for that project, in writing. Price moves only if scope moves.

Do I have to change my software?

No. The crew works inside the email, files and calendar you already use. Nothing is moved or replaced, and it starts with read access.

Does the AI send anything without me?

No. Every email, price, signature and payment waits for your yes. The crew drafts. You decide.

Is this for my company?

It's built for general contractors, remodelers and custom builders, specialty contractors, and GCs bidding public work. Not sure? Call 646-281-8368.
