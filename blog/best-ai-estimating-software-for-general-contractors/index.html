Best AI estimating software for general contractors (2026)

Prices and features checked October 16, 2026 (ET). BuildWithBNZ publishes this page and is one of the options listed. We've tried to be fair: every competitor price links to its source, and anything we couldn't confirm is marked.

The short answer: There's no single best AI estimating software for general contractors. Pick by your bottleneck:

  • Need faster takeoff your estimators run themselves? Togal or Kreo.
  • Want takeoffs done for you? Beam AI (Attentive.ai).
  • Want AI takeoff and an estimate in one place, by trade? Bobyard.
  • Residential builder or remodeler? Handoff.
  • Need the estimate built and the subs chased, inside Google Workspace? BuildWithBNZ, an AI estimating crew for general contractors.

What "AI estimating" means here

Most tools sold as AI estimating are really AI takeoff: they measure areas, lengths and counts on plans. A smaller group also prices the estimate. Fewer still touch sub bids: invitations, follow-up, bid leveling, and COIs. When you compare estimating software for general contractors, check which of those three jobs each tool actually does.

Togal.AI

What it is: cloud takeoff that uses AI to measure areas, walls and counts on PDF plans. Price: Growth is $299/month per user, billed yearly. Business is custom-priced for 4+ users and adds SSO, onboarding and a dedicated rep (Togal, Oct 16, 2026). Fits: GCs who need quick quantities and finish trades working off clean architectural sets. Limits: A 2026 peer-reviewed study found results within 5% of On-Screen Takeoff on floor plans after manual adjustment, with weaker results on scanned drawings (ASCE). Its pricing page lists takeoff features, not estimate pricing or sub outreach. More in our Togal review.

Kreo

What it is: a takeoff and estimating tool. Essential has no AI. Pro adds AI takeoff (rooms, walls, doors, windows), auto symbol counting and an AI assistant called Caddie. Pricing and live-linked estimates are included (Kreo, Oct 16, 2026). Price: see Kreo's pricing page for current plans and terms; we haven't confirmed them. Fits: estimating teams that want takeoff and a priced estimate in one tool, with a published price. Limits: annual billing only. Sub bids and invitations aren't listed. We found no independent accuracy review to cite as of Oct 16, 2026.

Beam AI (Attentive.ai)

What it is: AI takeoffs, either do-it-yourself software or "done for you" by Beam's team with human review (Beam AI, Oct 16, 2026). Price: sold as annual licenses by trade and volume; see Beam AI's pricing page for current figures, which we haven't confirmed. Fits: firms that would rather outsource takeoff than run software, and are bidding steady volume. Limits: annual licenses are a larger upfront commitment. Beam claims "99%+ accurate" done-for-you takeoffs. That's a vendor claim we couldn't check independently.

Bobyard

What it is: AI takeoff with models trained per trade, flowing into an estimate table, so there's no export step (Bobyard, read Oct 16, 2026). Price: not published. Bobyard describes a flat per-estimator-seat subscription, a "premium subscription priced for serious commercial bid volume," with a customer success manager included (Bobyard). Ask sales for a quote. Fits: trade contractors (Bobyard names landscaping, electrical and finishes) and teams that want takeoff and estimate in one workflow. Limits: no public price. Confirm it covers your trades before you buy.

Handoff

What it is: AI estimating for residential builders and remodelers, with costs Handoff says update daily by US city (Handoff, Oct 16, 2026). Price: see Handoff's pricing page for current plans and terms; we haven't confirmed them. Fits: residential GCs, custom builders and remodelers. Limits: built for residential. Commercial GCs should look elsewhere. Ask about contract terms before you buy.

BuildWithBNZ

What it is: not a tool you run. It's three AI workers for construction estimating and sub outreach, inside your own Google Drive, Gmail, Google Calendar and Notion:

  • Taylor reads drawings, specs, addenda, the wage schedule and the bid form, does the takeoff, flags conflicts, and prices labor, material and subs line by line. Every line shows its source, and Taylor writes UNKNOWN instead of guessing.

Price: no price list. One set price per project, in writing, after a Project Review, then a separate monthly arrangement (FAQ). Fits: GCs who want to bid more work without hiring estimators, already run on Google Workspace, and bid public or prevailing-wage work. Limits:

  • Procore, QuickBooks, Bluebeam, PlanSwift/STACK, Excel, Microsoft 365 and others are listed as "Next," not live.
  • No published monthly price to compare.
  • No published accuracy or turnaround results yet.

How to pick the best AI estimating software for general contractors

  1. Name the bottleneck. Takeoff hours, pricing, or chasing subs?
  2. Test on your own drawings, including a scanned set and your hardest trade.
  3. Check what it exports to. Your estimate has to end up somewhere your team trusts.
  4. Get the price in writing, per user and per year, with renewal terms.
  5. Count the whole bid, not just takeoff. Sub coverage and bid leveling decide bid day as much as quantities do.

When you're weighing an AI construction estimator, a short pilot on one live bid tells you more than any roundup, this one included.
