# Notion for construction companies

**The short answer:** A lot of contractors keep their sub list and project notes in Notion because it's flexible and cheap to set up. The problem is keeping it current. BuildWithBNZ reads and updates your Notion project notes and sub network in place. Emma logs who quoted, who answered on time and whose paperwork is clean, so the database gets better every bid instead of going stale.

## Why Notion sub lists go stale

Someone builds a nice subcontractor database. It has trades, contacts, service area, maybe a rating. Six months later half the contacts have changed, nobody logged which subs no-bid the last three jobs, and estimators are back to texting the same five people.

The database isn't the problem. Updating it after every bid is.

## What the crew does in Notion

- **Emma reads your sub network** when she builds the list for a bid, to match your niche, geography and job size.
- **Emma updates it after each bid:** quote received or not, on time or not, COI and W-9 status.
- **Sara reads and updates project notes,** so the project page reflects what was verified versus what was only reported. Sara marks every claim Verified or Reported and never blends the two.

## What you control

- **Nothing moved or replaced.** Your workspace stays laid out the way you built it.
- **Scope.** Access follows the scope document for that project.
- **Approvals.** Anything that goes out to a sub still waits in Sara's approvals queue.

## Works with the rest of the crew

- **Gmail:** sub replies Emma reads become database updates. See [Gmail](/integrations/gmail/).
- **Google Drive:** the estimate and scope document live in Drive. See [Google Drive](/integrations/google-drive/).
- **Google Calendar:** quote promises and COI expirations. See [Google Calendar](/integrations/google-calendar/).

## FAQ

**Do I need Notion to use BuildWithBNZ?**
Notion is one of the systems the crew connects to. If you keep project notes or a sub list in Notion, the crew reads and updates them in place.

**Will BuildWithBNZ restructure my Notion workspace?**
No. Notes and the sub network are read and updated in place.

**What goes into the subcontractor database?**
Who quotes, who answers on time, and whose paperwork is clean.
