Best AI for contractors (2026): estimating, bids and subs

Prices and features checked October 16, 2026 (ET). BuildWithBNZ publishes this page and is one of the options listed. Every competitor price links to its source. Anything we couldn't confirm is marked.

The short answer: The best AI for contractors is the one that takes your slowest job off your desk. For most contractors, that job is one of four things: takeoff, pricing the estimate, sending bid invites, or chasing subs. No single tool on this list does all four the same way. So start with the job, not the brand.

  • Fast takeoff you run yourself: Togal.AI or Kreo.
  • Takeoff done for you: Beam AI.
  • Residential estimating: Handoff.
  • Bid packages and invites: Downtobid.
  • The estimate and the sub chasing done in your own Gmail and Drive: BuildWithBNZ, an AI crew for contractors.

The four jobs AI can help with

A few terms first, in plain words:

  • Takeoff means measuring the plans. You count doors, measure walls and figure square feet.
  • Estimate means putting prices on those quantities: labor, material and subs.
  • An ITB (invitation to bid) is the email that asks a sub to price part of the job.
  • Bid leveling means lining up sub quotes so they cover the same scope before you compare prices.
  • A COI is a certificate of insurance. A W-9 is the tax form you collect from each sub.

Most AI tools for contractors do one of these jobs well. A few try to do two. Very few touch the sub side.

Takeoff: Togal.AI and Kreo

Togal.AI uses AI to measure areas, walls and counts on PDF plans. Growth costs $299 a month per user, billed yearly (Togal, Oct 16, 2026). A 2026 peer-reviewed study found it within 5% of On-Screen Takeoff on floor plans after manual fixes. Results were weaker on scanned drawings (ASCE). Its pricing page lists takeoff features, not estimate pricing or sub outreach.

Kreo does takeoff and estimating in one tool. Its Essential plan has no AI. Pro adds AI takeoff and an AI assistant called Caddie. For current plans and terms, see Kreo's pricing page. We found no independent accuracy review to cite as of Oct 16, 2026.

Takeoff done for you: Beam AI

Beam AI (from Attentive.ai) sells takeoff software or takeoffs done by its own team with human review. It's sold as annual licenses by trade and volume. See Beam AI's pricing page for current figures. Beam says its done-for-you takeoffs are "99%+ accurate." That's a vendor claim we couldn't check on our own.

It fits firms that bid steady volume and would rather hand off takeoff than run software.

Residential estimating: Handoff

Handoff is AI estimating for residential builders and remodelers. For current plans and terms, see Handoff's pricing page. Ask about contract terms before you buy. It's built for residential work, so commercial GCs should look elsewhere.

Bid packages and invites: Downtobid

Downtobid reads plan sets, sorts the work into trade bid packages and drafts scope notes. Then it sends invitations from your own email (downtobid.com). GC plans start at $149 a month per organization. Subs pay $119 a month for a leads bid board or $299 a month for full bid management, and can bid free (downtobid.com, homepage FAQ, Oct 16, 2026). Ask about billing and contract terms before you buy.

The estimate and the subs: BuildWithBNZ

BuildWithBNZ isn't a tool you run. It's three AI workers inside your own Google Drive, Gmail, Google Calendar and Notion:

  • Taylor reads drawings, specs, addenda (changes issued after the plans go out) and the bid form. Taylor does the takeoff, flags conflicts and prices the job line by line. Every line shows its source. Where information is missing, Taylor writes UNKNOWN instead of guessing.
  • Emma drafts sub invitations in your Gmail, follows up, and collects quotes, COIs and W-9s. Then she levels each quote against Taylor's estimate.
  • Sara keeps one approvals queue. Nothing goes out until you approve it.

If you want an AI assistant for contractors that does the bid work, not just speeds it up, this is the option to look at.

How to pick the best AI for contractors like you

  1. Name your slowest job. Is it takeoff hours, pricing, sending invites or chasing subs?
  2. Test on your own drawings. Include a scanned set and your hardest trade.
  3. Check where the work ends up. Does it land in a tool your team already uses?
  4. Get the price in writing. Ask per user, per year and about renewal terms.
  5. Count the whole bid. Sub coverage and leveling decide bid day as much as quantities do.

FAQ

What is the best AI for contractors? It depends on your slowest job. See the short answer at the top.

How much do AI tools for contractors cost? Togal.AI Growth is $299/month per user, billed yearly. Downtobid starts at $149/month per GC organization (both from the vendors' own sites, Oct 16, 2026). For others, check each vendor's pricing page.

Can an AI estimator for contractors replace my estimator? Not today. Use AI to get more bids out, and have a person check every number.

Do AI tools for contractors work with Gmail and Google Drive? Some do. Downtobid sends invites from your own email. BuildWithBNZ works inside Google Drive, Gmail, Google Calendar and Notion.
