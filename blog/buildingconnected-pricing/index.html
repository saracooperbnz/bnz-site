BuildingConnected pricing: what it costs and cheaper options

Facts checked October 16, 2026. Autodesk quotes by sales, so your price may differ.

The short answer: BuildingConnected pricing isn't public. Autodesk doesn't publish a price for BuildingConnected Pro (for GCs) or Bid Board Pro (for subs). Its pricing page describes "flexible user, project, and account-based pricing" and sends you to sales (Autodesk pricing page). BuildingConnected is quote-only: no price is published.

The products and what each is for

BuildingConnected is Autodesk's preconstruction product for GCs and subs (BuildingConnected help center, read Oct 16, 2026). Autodesk Construction Cloud is now part of Autodesk Forma, so you'll see both names.

Product Who it's for What Autodesk lists Price
BuildingConnected Pro GCs Access to "1.5+ million builders," unlimited bid invites and projects, bid leveling and cost comparison, custom bid forms, historical bid data and analytics Quoted by sales
Bid Board Subs Manage and submit bids, access project files, message GCs, submit qualifications Ask Autodesk
Bid Board Pro Subs Automated bid tracking, bid forwarding, company-wide views, calendar sync, bid analytics Quoted by sales
TradeTapp GCs Sub qualification: custom questionnaires, automated risk assessment, financial calculations, ERP integration Quoted by sales
Forma for Preconstruction (bundle) GCs Forma Takeoff, Forma Data Management, BuildingConnected Pro, Bid Board Pro, ProEst, TradeTapp at "one price" Quoted by sales

Sources: Autodesk pricing page and BuildingConnected support: requesting Pro pricing, both read Oct 16, 2026.

Also on Autodesk's pricing page: a 30-day money-back guarantee, support 24 hours a day Monday to Friday, and browser support for Chrome, Firefox and Safari.

BuildingConnected pricing: third-party estimates

Software directories and competitors publish price estimates for BuildingConnected, but none of them come from Autodesk, and they don't say which plan they cover. We've left them out. The only reliable number is the one in your written quote.

What drives your quote

Autodesk names users, projects and accounts as pricing factors (Autodesk). Before you take a call, know:

  • how many people will send invites or level bids
  • how many projects you bid per year
  • whether you need TradeTapp, takeoff or estimating too (the bundle may change the math)
  • how long a contract term you're willing to sign

Ask the rep for the price in writing, per user and per year, with renewal terms.

Who BuildingConnected fits

  • GCs who want a large network to invite from and a standard bidding portal subs already know.
  • Subs whose GCs already send invites through it. Many subs get invited there whether they pay or not.
  • Companies that need formal prequalification (TradeTapp) or are standardizing on Autodesk software.

Limits to know before you buy

  • No public price. You can't compare without a sales call.
  • It's a bid platform, not an estimator. Autodesk sells takeoff and estimating separately (Forma Takeoff, ProEst) or in the bundle (Autodesk).
  • Work happens in the platform. Invites, bids and messages live in BuildingConnected, not your own inbox.

Cheaper or different options

  • PlanHub: a third-party roundup describes it as free for GCs, with subs paying for lead access (StatWharf, checked Oct 11, 2026). Confirm on planhub.com.
  • Downtobid publishes starting prices: GC organization from $149/month (homepage FAQ, Oct 16, 2026). See our Downtobid pricing post.
  • A spreadsheet and your own email. Free, and it works at low volume. It breaks when follow-up slips.

Where BuildWithBNZ fits instead

BuildWithBNZ isn't a bidding network. It's three AI workers that do the bid work inside your own Gmail, Google Drive, Google Calendar and Notion:

  • Taylor builds the estimate from the bid package, with every source shown, and writes UNKNOWN instead of guessing.
  • Emma builds your sub list for your trade and area, drafts invitations in your Gmail with the right files, follows up, collects quotes, COIs and W-9s, and levels scopes against Taylor's estimate.
  • Sara keeps one approvals queue. Nothing goes to a sub until you approve it.

Pick BuildingConnected if you need the network, your subs and GCs already live there, or you need TradeTapp-style prequalification.

Look at BuildWithBNZ for bid management if your bottleneck is the estimate and the chasing, not finding subs, and you'd rather work from your own inbox than another portal.

FAQ

How much does BuildingConnected cost? No public price. BuildingConnected is quote-only (Autodesk pricing page, Oct 16, 2026).

Is BuildingConnected free for subcontractors? Subs can use Bid Board to manage and submit bids. Bid Board Pro is quote-only. Ask Autodesk what's included free.

What is Forma for Preconstruction? Autodesk's bundle of BuildingConnected Pro, Bid Board Pro, TradeTapp, Forma Takeoff, ProEst and Forma Data Management at one price.
