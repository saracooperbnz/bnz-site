# Construction estimating software for small contractors

*Prices checked October 16, 2026 (ET). Vendors change plans often, so confirm before you buy.*

**The short answer:** If you bid a handful of jobs a month, start with a **free plan** (such as STACK Free; Buildxact also lists one) or the PDF tool you already have. Move to a paid tool when takeoff hours or missed scope start costing you work. Paid plans vary by vendor, plan and seat count, so check each vendor's pricing page and ask about the contract term. Pick by your work type: residential builders and remodelers lean toward Buildxact or Handoff; trade contractors and small commercial GCs lean toward STACK, PlanSwift or Kreo.

## What small contractors should check first

A checklist before any demo:

- [ ] **Work type fit.** Residential remodels, new homes, trade packages or commercial bids? Tools are built for one more than the others.
- [ ] **Takeoff *and* pricing.** Some tools only measure. Look for automated construction estimating, where quantities flow into pricing, if you don't want to retype them into Excel.
- [ ] **Your own costs.** Can you load your labor rates and supplier prices, or are you stuck with a national database?
- [ ] **Mac or Windows.** PlanSwift is described as Windows-based ([ConstructConnect](https://www.constructconnect.com/blog/ai-powered-takeoff-and-estimating-software-a-contractors-guide-to-the-top-players-in-2026)). STACK, Buildxact and Kreo are cloud-based.
- [ ] **Term.** Almost everything above is a 12-month commitment. Run the free plan or trial on a real bid first.
- [ ] **Users.** Per-user pricing (STACK, Kreo, PlanSwift) vs. company-wide plans changes the math once a second person estimates.
- [ ] **Proposal output.** Can you send a clean proposal to the owner straight from the tool?

## Quick picks by situation

- **Residential builder or remodeler, 1–3 people:** Buildxact or Handoff Flex. Both are built for residential. For Buildxact's current plans, see [buildxact.com/us/pricing](https://www.buildxact.com/us/pricing/). Handoff Scale's AI takeoff covers residential plans up to 7,000 sq ft ([Handoff](https://www.handoff.ai/pricing)).
- **Trade sub bidding a few jobs a month:** STACK Free to start, then STACK Pro or ConstructConnect Takeoff Starter.
- **Small commercial GC who prices in Excel:** Kreo Essential (takeoff with live-linked estimates, no AI) or a PDF tool like Bluebeam. See [PlanSwift vs Bluebeam](/blog/planswift-vs-bluebeam/).
- **Want AI takeoff?** See our [best AI estimating software for general contractors](/blog/best-ai-estimating-software-for-general-contractors/) roundup.

## The part software doesn't do

These tools make the estimator faster. They don't send sub invitations, chase quotes, collect insurance certificates (see our [COI tracking](/guides/subcontractor-coi-tracking/) guide) or level sub numbers (see [leveling sub bids](/guides/bid-leveling/)). In a small company that's often the same person who does the estimate, and it's where nights and weekends go.
