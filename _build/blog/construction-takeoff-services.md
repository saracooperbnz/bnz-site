# Construction takeoff services vs AI takeoff: outsourcing or software?

*Checked October 17, 2026 (ET). BuildWithBNZ publishes this page. It isn't a takeoff service or a takeoff tool you run; we explain what it is below.*

**The short answer:** Construction takeoff services are outside teams that measure your plans for you. AI takeoff software is a tool your own estimator runs, with AI doing a first pass. A service saves your team's time but costs more and adds a hand-off. Software costs less but still needs someone in-house to run it and check it. The right pick depends on your bid volume and who you have on staff.

A few terms first:

- **Takeoff** means measuring the plans. You count doors, measure walls and figure square feet.
- **Addenda** are changes to the plans or specs issued after the bid package goes out.
- **Turnaround** is how long it takes to get the takeoff back.

## How construction takeoff services work

Most takeoff services follow the same steps:

1. You send the plans, specs and addenda, plus the scope you want measured.
2. The service measures the job. Some use AI tools with human review.
3. You get quantities back, often in a spreadsheet.
4. You price the job and build your bid.

The service measures. You still price. A takeoff isn't an estimate.

**Example:** Beam AI sells "Done For You" takeoffs. Its pricing page says they are "QA-checked" and delivered in "24-72 hours" ([Beam AI](https://www.ibeam.ai/pricing), Oct 17, 2026). It sells annual licenses. Done For You starts at $25K a year, "based on trade and volume." Those are the vendor's own claims and prices. Beam also appears in [our guide to AI tools for contractors](/blog/best-ai-for-contractors/).

## How AI takeoff software works

With software, your estimator does the takeoff, and AI speeds up the boring parts.

**Example:** PlanSwift's Core plan includes Auto Takeoff. PlanSwift says it "builds a first-pass takeoff for areas, lengths, and counts in minutes." Core is $3,000 a year for 1 seat ([PlanSwift](https://www.planswift.com/pricing/), Oct 17, 2026).

"First pass" matters. The AI makes a draft. Your estimator still checks it, fixes it and adds what it missed. For a closer look at one AI takeoff tool, see [our Togal AI review](/blog/togal-ai-review/).

## Construction takeoff services cost vs software cost

Prices vary a lot by provider, trade and volume. So compare how you pay, not just the number.

| | Takeoff service | AI takeoff software |
|---|---|---|
| How you pay | Per project, or an annual license (varies by provider) | Per seat, usually per year |
| Who does the work | The service's team | Your estimator, with AI help |
| Your time | Send plans, review results | Run the takeoff, check it, fix it |
| Speed | Depends on the provider's queue | Depends on your estimator's time |
| Control | Less. You see the result, not every click | More. You see every measurement |
| Addenda | Send each change and wait for a revision | Update it yourself |

When you get a quote from a service, ask:

- Is it priced per project, per sheet or per year?
- What's the turnaround, and what happens when addenda drop late?
- Who checks the work, and how?
- What format do the quantities come back in?
- Which trades do you cover?

## When a takeoff service makes sense

- You bid more work than your team can measure.
- You don't have an estimator in-house, or yours is buried.
- You're bidding a trade you don't usually measure.
- You'd rather pay to get time back than learn new software.

Run an electrical shop? See [how electrical contractors weigh software against estimating services](/blog/estimating-software-for-electrical-contractors/). Roofers often order aerial measurement reports instead; see [roofing estimating software and report pricing](/blog/roofing-estimating-software/).

## When AI takeoff software makes sense

- You have an estimator with time to run it.
- You want to see and control every measurement.
- Addenda come late and often, and you can't wait on a service.
- You bid steady work in the same trades, so templates pay off.

## Check every takeoff, no matter who does it

Whoever measures, you sign the bid. Before it goes out:

- Spot-check the three biggest quantities by hand.
- Make sure the latest addenda were used.
- Look for scope that falls between trades.
- Compare the totals to a similar job you've built.

## FAQ

**What are construction takeoff services?**
Outside companies that measure your plans and send back quantities for you to price.

**How much do construction takeoff services cost?**
It varies by provider, trade and volume. For example, Beam AI lists done-for-you takeoffs from $25K a year (Oct 17, 2026). Get quotes for the same job.

**Is AI takeoff software cheaper than a takeoff service?**

Yes. Spot-check the biggest quantities and confirm the latest addenda were used.
