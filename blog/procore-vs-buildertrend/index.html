Procore vs Buildertrend: which fits your company?

Both are construction management platforms with unlimited users, and neither publishes a price. Procore charges an annual fee per product, scaled to your Annual Construction Volume (ACV) (Procore pricing). It's the common pick for commercial GCs running many stakeholders on one project. Buildertrend gives a custom quote from a short questionnaire (Buildertrend pricing) and is widely used by residential builders and remodelers. Pick on who your clients are and how much construction you run, then compare written quotes.

Procore: what to know

  • ACV is the meter. Procore defines it as "the aggregate dollar value of the construction work across your projects" (Procore). Price goes up as volume goes up.
  • Unlimited users means you can bring in subs, owners and architects without paying per seat.
  • Ways to manage lumpy volume: multi-year pools, pre-priced extra volume and locked renewal rates (Procore blog).
  • Limits:

- No public price, so you can't compare without a sales call.
- It's priced by product, so adding modules (for example estimating or bid management) adds cost.
- TrustRadius lists no free version or trial (TrustRadius).

Buildertrend: what to know

  • Custom quote. Buildertrend's page asks "a few quick questions," then you schedule a call (Buildertrend).
  • Former published tiers are gone. A competitor's analysis says Buildertrend dropped Essential/Advanced/Complete pricing in early 2025. The last published rates (late 2024) were $449, $719 and $989/month billed annually (Projul; Projul is a competitor). Treat those as history, not a quote.
  • A reviewer's 2026 quote included a one-year minimum, no setup fee, and a required support tier at $100/month (Tier 1) or $300/month (Tier 2) (Connecteam, 2026). Ask whether a paid support tier is required for you.
  • Small-company fit: the same reviewer said picking the $0–$499,000 volume option got a message that Buildertrend may not be the best fit for smaller businesses (Connecteam).
  • Limits:

- No public price.
- Reported annual minimum.

Quick checklist

  • Commercial with lots of outside stakeholders? Get a Procore quote.
  • Residential builder or remodeler with homeowner clients? Get a Buildertrend quote.
  • Know your annual construction volume before either call. Both ask about it.
  • List the modules you actually need. Procore prices per product.
  • Ask about term, renewal increases and required support fees, in writing.
  • Still writing the weekly owner report by hand? Neither platform writes it for you; see construction weekly report automation.
  • Check where estimating and sub paperwork live. Tracking subs (for example, track sub COIs) may need its own process either way.

FAQ

Asked on every call.

How is Procore priced? +
Procore charges an upfront annual fee by product, based on your Annual Construction Volume (the dollar value of construction work across your projects), with unlimited users. It does not publish rates; you need a quote.
How much does Buildertrend cost in 2026? +
Buildertrend no longer publishes prices. Its pricing page asks a few questions about your business and gives a custom quote, with unlimited users included. Third-party reviewers report quotes tied to annual construction volume.
Is Procore or Buildertrend better for a small contractor? +
Neither publishes a price, so get quotes from both. One reviewer reported Buildertrend's quote form suggested it may not be the best fit for companies under $500,000 in annual volume.
