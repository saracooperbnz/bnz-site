Why there's no price on this page

There is no standard construction project, so there is no standard price. A remodel with three trades, a commercial fit-out and a prevailing-wage public job ask very different things of Taylor (estimating), Emma (subs) and Sara (operations). A per-seat or per-month sticker price would either overcharge the small job or quietly cut corners on the big one.

So we price the work in front of us, and we put it in writing.

The three steps

1. Project Review

A real call about one real project. You bring the drawings or the bid package and tell us who's on the job. We walk through what the crew would do with it: what Taylor would take off and price, which trades Emma would invite, and what Sara would put in front of you to approve.

  • What you get: a written walkthrough of your project.
  • What you control: you pick the project. Nothing is connected to your accounts yet.

Book a Project Review

2. Scope and one set price, in writing

If it makes sense to go ahead, we send a scope document for that project. It says:

  • what the crew will do (for example, Taylor's estimate, Emma's sub invitations and quote leveling, Sara's approvals queue and Monday report)
  • what the crew will not do
  • what stays with you: every approval, every send, the signature
  • one set price for that project

Not a plan, not a tier. It's a document you can hold us to. The price moves only if the scope moves. If you add a trade or a second bid package, we write up the change before the work starts.

3. The monthly arrangement

After the first project, the crew can stay on under a separate monthly arrangement. It covers:

  • hosting and monitoring of the crew
  • new projects as they come in
  • a report every Monday: active projects, estimates issued, quotes collected, what's waiting on you and what's waiting on subs

We stay responsible for it working.

What you're not paying for

  • New software to learn. The crew works inside Google Drive, Gmail, Google Calendar and Notion. Nothing is moved or replaced. See the tools we connect to.
  • Integrations that aren't live. Procore, Buildertrend, QuickBooks, Sage, Bluebeam, PlanSwift/STACK, DocuSign, Microsoft 365, Dropbox and Excel are listed as "Next." They are not live today and are not part of any current price.

Who owns the work

The estimate, the scope document and the Monday report land as files in your own Google Workspace. Access is scoped per project. A company that hires us for one job doesn't open its whole Drive.

How this compares to other tools

Many construction software companies sell recurring software plans or quote through a sales team. Some publish prices in their FAQ. We price per project because the crew does the work on the project, not just provides a tool.
